I want to keep track of my family's money. I get a salary every month, and I want to record each payment: the date, the amount and who paid it.

I also want to record our household spending. Each spend has a date, an amount, a short note and a category such as groceries, rent, school fees or travel. I want to see totals by category.

We have two bank accounts, a savings account and a salary account. Each account has a name and the bank it is with. Salary goes into one account, and every spend is paid from one of them.

Sometimes I lend money to my brother. I want to note each loan with the date and amount, and then each time he pays some back, with the date and amount, so I can see how much he still owes me.
