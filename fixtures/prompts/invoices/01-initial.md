I run a small consulting business and want to keep track of my clients and invoices.

Each client has a name, an email address and a billing address. Each invoice belongs to one client and has an invoice number, an issue date and a due date.

An invoice has several line items. Each line item has a description, a quantity and a unit price.

Clients pay me, sometimes in parts. I want to record each payment received against an invoice, with the date, the amount and how it was paid (bank transfer, cash or card). I want to see how much of each invoice is still unpaid.
